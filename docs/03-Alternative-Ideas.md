# Backup ideas (if LUSONG does not fit your team or your region's problem statement)

## A. GURO-LIBRE: AI that fills DepEd forms from a teacher's class record photo
- Problem: 875,514 public school teachers; 42% work 50+ hours a week, 17.8 hours a week on ancillary tasks; DepEd still requires 5 core forms plus dozens of role and teaching forms despite DO 002 s.2024.
- Solution: a mobile app that photographs the teacher's handwritten or spreadsheet class record and auto-generates School Forms (SF1, SF2, SF5, SF9, SF10) and program reports, with on-device OCR and a Filipino-language assistant; exports in DepEd LIS format.
- Why it could win: enormous, measurable time savings (hours per week per teacher); judges and mentors know the pain personally; pure software; SDG 4.c.
- Risks: LIS integration and DepEd data rules; edtech is a crowded category so position strictly as teacher admin, not learning.

## B. TOTOO-BA: satellite plus citizen verification of public infrastructure projects
- Problem: DPWH found 472 "ghost" flood control projects by November 2025; PHP 545 billion spent on flood control 2022 to 2025 while flood-prone areas remain unprotected.
- Solution: a platform that takes the DPWH project list, pulls free Sentinel-2 satellite imagery before and after the claimed completion date, flags projects with no visible change, and dispatches geotagged citizen photo verification through barangay volunteers; outputs audit-ready reports for COA, the Independent Commission for Infrastructure, and LGUs.
- Why it could win: extremely timely, frontier tech (satellite change detection) that students can build with free data, SDG 16.5 and 16.6, 11.5, 13.1.
- Risks: political sensitivity in a government-run competition; frame it as a tool for agencies to verify contractors, not to accuse officials. Demo is strong (before and after imagery on stage).

## C. TUBIG-TIPID: non-revenue water detection for small water districts
- Problem: only 149 of 532 water districts (28%) keep non-revenue water at or under the 20% ceiling; some provincial districts lose 40 to 50% of treated water.
- Solution: low-cost pressure and flow sensors (ESP32 + LoRa) plus an AI leak-localization dashboard sold to the 380 districts above the ceiling, with a pay-from-savings model.
- Why it could win: huge, quantifiable savings; SDG 6.4; IoT fits the PSC XI theme list.
- Risks: hardware entries have placed 2nd to 4th but have not won nationally in recent years; pilots take longer than 8 weeks.

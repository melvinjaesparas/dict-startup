# Competition Brief: DICT Philippine Startup Challenge XI (2026)

Compiled 2026-10-11 from public sources. The shared startup.gov.ph news link could not be opened from this environment, so verify dates and the official 2026 mechanics with your DICT Regional Office or psc_inquiries@dict.gov.ph.

## What it is
- Annual startup pitching competition of the DICT ICT Industry Development Bureau (IIDB), under RA 11337 (Innovative Startup Act).
- 2026 is the 11th edition (PSC XI). PSC X ran in 2025 (regional rounds Oct–Nov, national finals Dec 3–4 in Clark). PSC 9 finals were Dec 3, 2024 in Tagaytay.
- Three stages: Regional Pitching Competition (DICT Regional Offices) → Semifinals → National Finals. Regional champions advance automatically; 2nd/3rd placers compete for remaining semifinal slots.

## Who can join (recent editions)
- Student teams, senior high or college: 3 students + 1 mentor (max 4). At least one female member encouraged.
- Startups from ideation to minimum viable product. Must be software or IoT-enabled.

## What to submit (from past calls plus the 2026 template)
- Registration via the DICT Regional Office form
- Concept Note (new emphasis in 2026) using the 10-section template: Summary; Background of the Problem; Proposed Startup Solution (with SDG priority); Objectives; Target Market/Beneficiaries; Value Proposition; Business Model; Market Analysis; Operations Plan; Financial Requirement. File name pattern: `TeamName_Concept Note_PSC XI` (confirm exact suffix with your regional office).
- Pitch deck (PDF), video pitch, startup logo, school clearance.

## How it is judged
- Regional (PSC 8 MIMAROPA mechanics): Viability 25%, Relevance 25%, Impact 20%, Creativity 20%, Presentation 10%.
- National (PSC 5): Customer validation 40%, Execution and design 30%, Business model validation 30%.
- Special awards: Best Pitch Video (audio/visual quality weighted 30%), Best Logo, Best Pitch.

## What has won before (avoid duplicating)
- PSC 4: anti-shortchanging app. PSC 5: SLU Team Victorem. PSC 8: Clarity, an AI-powered coding tool (USL Tuguegarao).
- PSC 9 top 10: Joltz, OneTake, Swine Scan, Kara, FISHNET, Bantay Baka, iWeave, Zeus X, STELLA.ai, SmartPosture. CALABARZON champ: konek.dev (legal help).
- PSC X regional champions: TrashScan (FEU Tech, NCR, waste), Signnify (Bicol U, sign language), OA Knee-mo! (Ateneo de Zamboanga, biomedical), UBRA (USL Tuguegarao, Region 2).

## 2026 tailwinds
- Philippines chairs ASEAN in 2026; DICT chairs the Innovative Startup Act Steering Committee; Philippine Startup Week 2026 is aligned with ASEAN digital priorities.
